# Community Code of Conduct

مجتمع سَنَد يرحّب بالجميع. ناقش الأفكار والشفرة باحترام، واحفظ خصوصية المشاركين. تنطبق القواعد على Issues وPull Requests والمناقشات ومساحات المشروع التي يديرها المشرف.

## Our standard

Be constructive, considerate and specific. Welcome different skill levels and backgrounds. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Respect Arabic and English participation, acknowledge mistakes and help others reproduce or understand a problem.

We do not accept harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, doxxing, publication of private information without permission, spam, malicious links or repeated disruption after a maintainer asks it to stop. A disagreement about a feature is not a reason to target its author.

## Scope and moderation

These rules cover project-managed repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions, and conduct presented as official representation of Sanad. They do not appoint the project as an arbiter of unrelated private disputes.

The maintainer may request an edit, remove disruptive content, close or lock a thread, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response should consider severity, impact, repetition and available evidence. Where practical and compatible with privacy, the maintainer explains the reason. No one may retaliate against a good-faith reporter.

## Report a concern privately

Email **IM_MURAD7@hotmail.com**, subject `Sanad community conduct`. Describe the relevant thread, behavior, dates and supporting evidence. Send only information necessary to understand the report; do not post personal details publicly.

The current maintainer is [Murad Aljohani](https://github.com/muradaljohani). If a report concerns the maintainer, you can also use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). GitHub operates its own moderation process.

Reports are handled with care and information is limited to what is necessary to investigate or respond. Absolute confidentiality, a staffed response team and a fixed response time are not promised. A reporter may request reconsideration by replying with relevant new context.

For a software vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public conduct report. For an immediate personal emergency, use appropriate local emergency services; the project tracker is not an emergency service.
